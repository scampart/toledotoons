Logo rules

  1. Preserve the lockup. Keep the frog, drawing, Toledo script and TOONS in their supplied positions. Scale the whole logo proportionally.
  2. Keep the white details. The smile border flows into the angular white chest shown in the reference. Do not round it into an oval belly. Eyes, drawing paper and TOONS interiors stay white.
  3. Protect the silhouette. Keep the corrected angled body section beneath the arm, the forward-facing eyes and straight marker.
  4. Clear space. Leave at least half the height of a TOONS capital around every outer edge of the artwork, measured from the artwork rather than its artboard.
  5. Background. This master includes a white background. Use a white panel on purple, photos or dark artwork, with the same clear space.
  6. Starting minimum size. Use the full detailed logo at 240 px wide digitally or 50 mm wide in print. These are working recommendations; check a proof at actual size. Smaller icons need a separately simplified mascot.
  7. Keep it flat. No stretching, rotation, shadows, gradients, glows, recoloring or added outlines. Do not replace either word with a similar font.
  8. Supporting graphics. Use flat shapes without outlines for skyline, moon and scenery. Keep gray backgrounds quieter than the mascot.

Type & production

The logo lettering is vector artwork, not live font text. Use the supplied paths as the master. Supporting page text should use a clean sans serif, leaving the expressive script exclusive to the logo.

Open Toledo_Toons_Logo.svg directly in Illustrator to edit its paths, then save a working AI copy. All four logo colors are normalized to the HEX values above. This is a traced vector reconstruction of the cleaned artwork, not a manual geometric redraw.

Color values are RGB/sRGB for digital use. Have your printer convert through the intended print profile and proof teal and red before a production run. Preserve white ink areas when printing on colored materials.
